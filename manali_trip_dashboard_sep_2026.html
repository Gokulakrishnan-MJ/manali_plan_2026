Six days, paced for the mountains.

The first day stays light after an overnight bus, while Sep 7 provides a weather buffer for the main excursion.

Fri · Sep 4

Fly north

Chennai to Chandigarh. Dinner, transfer and overnight Volvo to Manali.

Sat · Sep 5

Arrive gently

Baggage drop, Hadimba Temple, Old Manali, Mall Road and cafés.

Sun · Sep 6

Big landscape day

Solang Valley, Atal Tunnel and Sissu, subject to road and weather conditions.

Mon · Sep 7

Flexible loop

Naggar, nearby villages, local attractions or weather-backup sightseeing.

Tue · Sep 8

Last full day

Relax, shop or add an activity. Take the overnight bus after dinner.

Wed · Sep 9

Buffer + fly home

Reach Chandigarh in the morning; preserve a large margin before 7:25 PM.

September is not a snow trip

Do not expect snow in Manali town. Plan for green valleys, mountain views, Solang, Sissu, Old Manali and café time; distant high peaks may still show ice or snow.

Connection rule

A mountain-road arrival and a flight should never be a tight same-day connection. The plan intentionally uses a Sep 8 overnight bus and a long Sep 9 airport buffer.
